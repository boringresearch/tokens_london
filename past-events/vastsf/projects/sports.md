Project description
Hype Agent is a video agent that turns a raw, wide-angle soccer clip into a highlight people want to watch.
The clip is ingested on VAST AI OS and indexed with NVIDIA's VSS blueprint, which gives YOLO detections on every frame,
and Cosmos Reason reads the frames to find the story.
In our demo clip, Duke's #8 beats a defender one-on-one, strikes on frame 220 and scores past a diving keeper.
The agent tracks the ball on every frame and writes an edit plan: speed ramps, freeze frames, slow motion,
camera zooms and tilts, fire trails pinned to the real ball, player tags, text slams,
and sound effects synthesized from code and timed to the action.
It renders the cut, re-watches it frame by frame along with the audio waveform, critiques what's weak
(a slow opening, a muddy picture, dead air, cluttered text), and revises the plan.
After five loops a flat clip becomes a 19-second edit with a cold open,
a slow-motion replay and audio mastered to social-media loudness, with no human editor involved.

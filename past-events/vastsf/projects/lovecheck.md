Project description
LoveCheck is a three-site safety board: warehouse, neighborhood, and dashcam. It looks at those cameras and shows a few clips where someone might be at risk.
The UI is Next.js. Flask talks to team Video Search with credentials kept on the server, so the browser never sees login tokens. It finds the latest footage, searches for hazards, drops near-duplicate chunks, and streams the clip through our own proxy.

Project description
VAST CAM connects video data extraction with camera-specific model training. A dataset agent queries VAST AI OS, reviews video evidence, and extracts short pre-event clips with boolean labels indicating whether a person–robot or person–forklift collision follows.
We then use this dataset for supervised fine-tuning of Qwen3.6-27B. The goal is to turn a short clip from a fixed warehouse camera into a probability of collision within the next few seconds. The model sees only the lead-up footage; future footage is used solely to determine the training label.

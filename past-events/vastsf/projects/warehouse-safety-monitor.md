Project description
Warehouse Safety Monitor turns warehouse camera footage into a ranked queue of near misses, blocked aisles, crowding and unsafe behavior. Each incident shows the clip, measured distances and a suggested fix. Hotspot heatmaps and an end-of-shift report help supervisors act.

Project description
Robotics research needs datasets with verifiable diversity across environments, objects and actions. MATCHA helps researchers turn video archives into curated, labeled datasets for training and evaluation. Researchers describe the footage they need in plain language, inspect matching clips and objects at scale, identify coverage gaps and overrepresented scenarios, and edit labels in bulk. By making dataset composition visible and measurable, MATCHA helps teams build balanced datasets and export versioned collections with clear annotations and documented coverage.

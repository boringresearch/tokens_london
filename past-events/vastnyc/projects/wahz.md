### Technical architecture

do wahz What it does. wahz scores a live performance against the real record, note by note, for whatever instrument is in the room. Today that was voice through a USB mic and drums on MIDI pads, on Bullet With Butterfly Wings. You hear yourself with the record at under 60 ms, the chart scrolls over the music video, every syllable and hit is judged as you play, and the prize for a take is the music video with your face rendered onto the singer. How we did it.

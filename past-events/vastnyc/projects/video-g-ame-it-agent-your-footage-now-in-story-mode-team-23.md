Project description
Great stories are hiding in messy footage. VGA brings them into play with two creative teammates: a Context Agent that discovers meaningful moments and connects them into a story, and a Video Agent that turns that story into a video. Wrapped in a retro game-console experience, VGA makes the journey from scattered clips to a finished story feel like a creative quest.
Press Start. Find your story. Bring it to life.

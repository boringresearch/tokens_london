Project description
2.5 million workplace injuries and illnesses were reported in the U.S. in 2024. Cameras already record warehouses, hospitals, factories, and construction sites — but investigating an incident still means searching through hours of footage. What if you could ask your cameras what happened and get proof?
RECEIPTS remembers what cameras see and tracks changes over time. Every answer comes with a timestamp and video clip. It knows when evidence is missing or outdated — there’s a difference between knowing it was safe and knowing it is safe. Ask what happened. See the evidence. Know if it’s still true.

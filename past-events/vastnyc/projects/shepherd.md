Project description
Shepherd: an AI night watch for small farms
Every video agent watches people, cars or forklifts. Nobody is watching the animals. Shepherd reads every clip a farm’s motion camera records and tells the farmer which ones matter, so no animal gets hurt because nobody was watching.
We ran it on 16 real clips from our own chicken coop in Fredon, NJ and build this app.

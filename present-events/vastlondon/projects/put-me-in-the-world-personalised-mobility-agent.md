Project description
Put Me In The World connects a personalised 3D avatar with real street-video evidence. A browser-based pose estimate can personalise illustrative body proportions. VAST retrieves timestamped street clips; existing Cosmos descriptions and YOLO detections provide scene evidence. Caption keywords suggest terrain and obstacles, while a live Cosmos call recommends the next scenario and a button runs the next search. Users explore five device concepts, including a hands-free bypass aid, with authored movement previews and transparent rule-based rankings. A connection panel explains how street constraints relate to movement and device review questions. Built with Claude Code and Cursor, Three.js, MediaPipe and a Flask backend on the Team 6 stack. Limits: the avatar is not a body scan; video is not reconstructed or registered in 3D; movements and rankings are illustrative, not validated biomechanics or clinical recommendations. W&B runtime logging is not claimed.

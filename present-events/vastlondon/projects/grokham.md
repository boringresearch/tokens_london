Project description
Grokham brings video search and live street intelligence into one interactive console.
Query indexed footage in plain language, play matching clips, and ask grounded follow-up questions. Detect objects with YOLO11s and follow partial vehicle tracks with 3D map overlays.
LIVE from NY streets: explore NYC camera feeds on an interactive 3D Google map, with linked camera selection, video, detections, and chat.
Our creative feature is inter-camera VLM agent communication: one camera reports what it sees; its two nearest neighbors reply from their own video summaries. Compact yellow speech bubbles and highlighted cameras make the exchange visible on the map.
Built on the challenge stack: VAST Data / NVIDIA VSS for indexed video retrieval, NVIDIA Cosmos for visual reasoning, CoreWeave-hosted GPU inference, and Cursor for development. A unified UI connects query, detection, partial tracking, maps, and camera-agent dialogue.

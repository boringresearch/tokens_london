Project description
Time Search lets you search camera recordings in plain English on top of VAST and Coreweave architecture. Describe someone's clothing, review likely matches, and follow their sightings across cameras on a shared timeline. Every result links to the original clip and timestamp, so you can check what happened without scrubbing through long recordings.

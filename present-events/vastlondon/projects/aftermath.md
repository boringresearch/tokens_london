Project description
It is a disaster-response app where AI agents analyse drone video of a disaster or war zone, each looking for one thing (trapped survivors, hazards, building damage or blocked roads), and a coordinator ranks what they find so the operator sees the most urgent alerts first, located on a live video feed and a 3D map.

Project description
ReStrike is an AI-powered tennis analysis tool that doesn't just show you what happened. It helps you explore what could have happened.  Here, we're using VAST to process tennis match footage and identify potential net errors.
Now, instead of just watching the mistake, we can explore different possibilities.
What if the player hit the ball a little higher? What if they made contact earlier? Or changed the speed?
Our simulator visualizes how these changes could affect the ball's trajectory—and whether it might clear the net.

Project description
Cysense turns cyclists’ cameras into shared road intelligence. As you ride, NVIDIA Cosmos watches your phone’s camera and Clover, your ride companion, speaks up about hazards: blocked bike lanes, roadworks, car doors and vehicles cutting close. Every report is pinned to the map with the video evidence behind it, and each one is tracked over time as it’s confirmed, goes stale or clears. Riders coming up behind get a heads-up or an automatic reroute before they reach it. In the recorded NYC replays, a hazard filmed by one bike changes the next rider’s route. In live mode, you search for a destination, Cysense draws a bike route with turn-by-turn directions, and Clover warns you and reroutes around blocked roads as you go. The stack is NVIDIA Cosmos for video understanding, VAST for the searchable footage archive, and W&B Weave tracing every model call.

Project description
🦮 Guidog — Your AI Guide, Right in Your Pocket👁️ The ProblemOver 2 million people in the UK live with sight loss. Navigating busy streets can be challenging, while guide dogs cost approximately £102,000 over their working lifetime. Existing AI wearables can be expensive and inaccessible.
What if greater independence was already in your pocket?
💡 Our SolutionGuidog turns your smartphone into an affordable AI mobility companion.
Using real-time computer vision, Guidog:
👁️ Sees ahead: Detects potential obstacles and hazards.
🔊 Speaks up: Delivers simple audio cues like "Stop!" or "Wait!"
📱 Costs less: Works with everyday smartphone hardware.
⚙️ How It Works📱 Camera → 🧠 AI Vision → 🔊 Audio Guidance → 🚶 Safer Decisions
🌍 Our VisionGuidog complements, rather than replaces, guide dogs and white canes. Our mission is to make AI-powered mobility assistance accessible to everyone.
🦮 Guidog — More awareness. More confidence. More independence.
